# MIMS QA User Guide — Customer & Savings Account Management

**Author:** Sameera (Owner: Branch, Agent, Customer & Account Screens)  
**System:** B-Trust Microbanking and Interest Management System (MIMS)  
**Target Audience:** QA Testers and Branch Agents  

---

## 1. Overview & Core Rules

MIMS is the central banking application for B-Trust microfinance. All business rules (business hours, age limits, minimum deposits, interest calculation) live directly inside the MySQL database via stored procedures and triggers.

### Key Operational Rules:
- **Business Hours:** Account opening, deposits, and withdrawals are permitted only **Monday to Friday, 09:00 – 16:00** (database server time). Requests outside business hours will be rejected by database triggers.
- **Role Modes:** Use the top header switcher to switch between **Agent View** (standard branch agent mode) and **Tester View** (QA mode with expanded debug error tracebacks).

## 2. Customer Management (`/customers`)

The Customers page allows branch agents and QA testers to search for existing customers, view customer account portfolios, or register new customers.

### 2.1 Searching Customers
1. Open **Customers** from the navigation bar or go to `http://localhost:5173/customers`.
2. Enter a search query in the search box (Name, NIC, Phone Number, or Customer ID).
3. The table displays up to 50 matching customer records with their Name, NIC, Age, Phone, and Assigned Branch.

### 2.2 Viewing Customer Profile & Accounts
1. Click **View** or select any customer from the search table.
2. The detailed profile panel displays:
   - Personal information (Customer ID, Full Name, NIC/ID, Date of Birth, Age, Address, Phone, Agent).
   - A complete list of all **Single** and **Joint** savings accounts owned by the customer, including account numbers, plan type, current balance, and account status.

### 2.3 Registering a New Customer
1. Click the **Register New Customer** button.
2. Fill in the required fields:
   - **First Name & Last Name** (Required)
   - **Date of Birth** (Required)
   - **Phone Number** (Required, 10 digits starting with `07`)
   - **Address** (Required)
   - **NIC / Identity Number:**
     - **Adults ($\ge 18$ years):** NIC is **mandatory**. Formats accepted: 9 digits followed by `V`/`X` (e.g., `901234567V`) or 12 digits (e.g., `199012345678`).
     - **Minors ($< 18$ years):** NIC is **optional**.
3. Click **Register Customer**.
4. Upon successful registration, the new Customer ID is generated by the database and displayed on screen.

## 3. Opening Savings Accounts (`/open-account`)

The Open Account page allows branch agents to open a new savings account for an existing customer.

### 3.1 Step-by-Step Account Opening Process
1. Navigate to **Open Account** (`http://localhost:5173/open-account`).
2. **Select Primary Customer:**
   - Use the **Customer Picker** field to type the customer's Name, NIC, or ID.
   - Click the matching customer from the dropdown list.
3. **Select Account Type:**
   - **Single Account:** Leave the Joint Account checkbox unchecked.
   - **Joint Account:** Check **Joint Account**, then use the secondary Customer Picker to choose the second account holder. *(Note: Primary and Joint holders must be different customers).*
4. **Plan Preview:**
   - The system automatically evaluates the customer's age and selects the appropriate savings plan:
     - **Minor Savings:** Age $< 18$ years | Minimum Deposit: **Rs. 500.00** | Interest Rate: **6.00% p.a.**
     - **Adult Savings:** Age $18 – 59$ years | Minimum Deposit: **Rs. 1,000.00** | Interest Rate: **5.00% p.a.**
     - **Senior Savings:** Age $\ge 60$ years | Minimum Deposit: **Rs. 1,000.00** | Interest Rate: **7.00% p.a.**
5. **Initial Deposit:**
   - Enter the opening deposit amount. Must be equal to or greater than the plan's minimum deposit.
6. Click **Open Account**.
7. The system invokes stored procedure `PROC_OPEN_SAVINGS_ACCOUNT` and displays the newly created Account Number.

## 4. QA Test Cases & Edge Cases to Validate

QA Testers should execute the following verification steps:

| Test Case | Steps | Expected Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Duplicate NIC** | Try registering a new customer with an NIC that already exists in sample data (e.g., `198512345678`). | Error 409 Conflict: *"A customer with this NIC is already registered."* |
| **Underage Adult ohne NIC** | Register a customer born 20 years ago without providing an NIC. | Form validation error: *"An adult customer must have an NIC."* |
| **Deposit Below Minimum** | Try opening an Adult Savings account with Rs. 200 initial deposit. | Validation error: *"Adult Savings requires an opening deposit of at least 1,000.00."* |
| **Outside Business Hours** | Change system clock outside Mon-Fri 09:00-16:00 and attempt to open an account. | Error 422: *"Deposits and new accounts are only accepted during business hours."* |
| **Joint Account Same Person** | Select Customer ID 1 as both Primary and Joint holder. | Validation error: *"Joint account holder must be a different customer."* |
